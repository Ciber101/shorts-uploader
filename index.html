Shorts Uploader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Shorts Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "CAI (AI Science Facts)" to upload his own original videos to that channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses. It uses YouTube API Services, with the owner's permission given through Google sign-in, to upload videos and set their title, description, and privacy status on the owner's own channel. It does not read, collect, or store data about any other YouTube user.

What it stores. It keeps only the sign-in token needed to upload to the owner's channel, on the owner's own computer. It does not sell, share, or send any data to third parties, and it uses no analytics or ads.

YouTube and Google. By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Removing access and data. Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is removed, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the owner's computer. Any stored data is deleted within 30 days of a request.
